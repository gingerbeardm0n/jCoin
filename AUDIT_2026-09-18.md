# jCoin audit — 2026-09-18

Goal judged against: an autonomous crypto-perpetuals bot on a US-legal Kraken venue, ready to trade ~$1k of real money and be profitable.

Method: 14 read-only scout runs (8 slices, 5 verifications, 1 rerun). Every high-severity item below was confirmed by two independent scouts or by running the code. Nothing was changed.

---

## 1. Verdict

Not on track. Three separate blockers, each alone enough to stop real-money trading:

1. **The live code points at a venue you cannot legally use.** It trades `futures.kraken.com` (offshore Kraken Futures), whose terms exclude US residents. The US-legal product, Kraken Derivatives US, is a different platform (CFTC-regulated, contracts on Bitnomial, launched June 2026), no public trading API was found, and 3 of our 9 coins (EGLD, SAND, IMX) are not listed on it.
2. **The "sealed" test window is not sealed.** At least ~1,000 config-by-coin evaluations touched the 2025–26 window, and the coin list was chosen by which coins passed it. The "validated" label carries little weight.
3. **Paper trading has not been continuous.** 38% of 4h bars were missed since the July reset, no alert reaches a human, and the 9/18 run was killed by a 10-minute scheduler timeout. Paper results are not the strategy's results.

The one thing that matters most: **decide the venue.** Until an API-accessible, US-legal perps venue is confirmed, every other improvement is polish on something that cannot go live.

Honest good news: on the only window nobody has selected on (2026-06-01 → 09-18, Kraken data, fresh $1k), the strategy was modestly positive for BTC+ETH (+$254, 18 trades, drawdown 4.9%). Small sample, one trade is 63% of it — but not a loser.

## 3. Fresh evidence: Kraken-data replay, unmodified engine

Full run Apr 2025 → halt (9 coins, $1k): peaked $1,418 (Nov 2025), **hard-halted at 40% drawdown on 2026-05-25, $868**. Per coin: IMX −206, AVAX −170, SAND −149, ADA −126, XRP −63, EGLD −44 | HBAR +198, BTC +201, ETH +205. Six of nine "validated" coins lost. BTC, which "failed" validation, was the second-best coin.

## 4. What we could do better (ranked by impact ÷ effort)

1. **Resolve the venue** (research, 1 session). Confirm whether Kraken Derivatives US exposes a trading API usable from Ohio. If not: Coinbase's US perps (has an official API; lists BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP), or Kraken spot long-only (loses shorts). Verify by placing one tiny real order via API, no VPN.
2. **Shrink universe to BTC+ETH** (1 hour). Kills the alt-drawdown, the unlisted-coin problem, and the contaminated screening program in one move. Verify: rerun the Jun–Sep replay.
3. **Declare 2026-06-01 onward the new sealed window** and write the rule down: no config or coin decision may look at it; every look gets logged in a file. (30 min.) Use the reset-replay script as the standing tracker.
4. **Add a notional cap + lot rounding + stop-failure halt** (half day). `size = min(risk_based, equity × max_lev / price)`, max_lev 1–2; `amount_to_precision` + min-size check; failed stop → flatten and latch. Verify with unit tests.
5. **Make paper trading continuous** (half day). Raise task timeout or cache the 2,190-bar cycle history; add a catch-up loop for missed bars; set a healthchecks.io ping with phone alert; fix watchdog battery flags; watch `kraken_state` too. Then either keep the PC awake or do GCP. Restart the paper clock that day.
6. **Model funding and real fee tier**, rerun (half day).
7. **One docs pass**: align spec ↔ code on risk %, universe, halts; delete the "8 trades" line; document the bear-phase scaling. (1 hour.)

## Venue research addendum (2026-09-18)

Follow-up venue research after the audit above, done specifically to move on action item #1
("resolve the venue"):

- **Kraken Derivatives US** (the actual US-legal Kraken perps product, CFTC-regulated,
  cleared via Bitnomial, launched June 2026) has, as far as this research found, **no
  trading API** — access is Kraken Pro app only. Its ETH contract size is 0.5 ETH
  (~$2,000 notional), with fees of $0.30/contract round trip. This does not fully resolve
  the open question from §6's blind spots (API absence found, not proven absent) — it
  strengthens rather than closes it.
- **Coinbase US nano perpetual-style futures** is the current leading candidate: nano BTC
  (0.01 BTC) and nano ETH (0.1 ETH) contracts, traded through the Advanced Trade API,
  CFTC-regulated. **This has not yet been verified with a real API test** — it is a
  candidate, not a decision, and should not be treated as settled until confirmed live
  from Ohio.
- Net effect on §1's verdict: the venue blocker is not resolved. It is narrowed to one
  concrete next step — confirm Coinbase's nano futures API with a real (tiny) order from
  Ohio, no VPN — rather than an open-ended "find a venue" search.
