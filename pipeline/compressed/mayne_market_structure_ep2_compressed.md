# Trader Mayne — Market Structure Ep2 (Compressed)

**Source:** YouTube whiteboard series, ep2 (market structure)
**Educator:** Trader Mayne

---

## Core Premise
Real price action (candle highs/lows) > arbitrary tools (trend lines, moving averages). Trend lines are imaginary overlays; high-low structure is what the market actually prints.

---

## Trend Phase Definitions

| Phase | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Uptrend | Sequence of higher highs (HH) AND higher lows (HL) | Buy |
| Downtrend | Sequence of lower lows (LL) AND lower highs (LH) | Sell |
| Consolidation | No clear directional sequence; price makes HH then LL or equal swings; messy, undirectional | Stand aside |

Rule: consolidation = don't trade. "If you just stop trading in these messy ranges, you don't have to worry about getting chopped and liquidated."

---

## Break of Structure (BOS)

- Definition: price closes past a prior swing high (bullish BOS) or prior swing low (bearish BOS).
- Terminology: Mayne uses "BOS" only. Does NOT use CHoCH, MSS, chalk.
- BOS confirms continuation of current trend direction.
- **Validation rule: candle BODY must close past the level. Wick rejections do NOT count as valid BOS.**

---

## Impulse vs Corrective Moves

**Impulse move (protrend):**
- Creates a BOS by closing past a prior swing high or low.
- Defines the trend direction.
- Can contain internal chop — still one impulse as long as it ultimately closes past the structural point.

**Corrective move (counter-trend):**
- Does NOT break any structural high or low.
- Trades within the range of the prior impulse (between its start and end point).
- Can be messy, multi-leg — still a correction as long as no closure past HH or LL.

Key rule: "Don't worry about the small chop. Focus on larger broad moves in the form of waves. View structure in this broader perspective."

---

## Internal Structure Trap (Critical Mistake)

- Within a corrective move, price can form apparent lower highs and lower lows (internal structure).
- These internal breaks do NOT signal trend reversal.
- Treating internal BOS as actual trend shift = major mistake; leads to selling bullish markets.
- Only a closure past the valid higher low (in uptrend) or lower high (in downtrend) changes trend classification.
- Safe substructure use: possible but requires additional context (covered in later episodes).

---

## Trend Reversal Signal

**Uptrend reversal:**
- Requires candle body closure BELOW the most recent validated higher low.
- This creates a lower low → immediate signal to expect lower high → sell.

**Downtrend reversal:**
- Requires candle body closure ABOVE the most recent validated lower high.
- This creates a higher high → expect higher low → buy.

---

## Trading With Structure — Entry Logic

- In uptrend: buy corrective pullbacks as close to the low as possible (above the validated HL).
- Entry zone: anywhere above the current validated HL, as low as physically possible.
- Do NOT buy when HH is printed; wait for corrective relief.
- Stop: just below the validated HL (that is the invalidation point).
- Target: next structural high.

In downtrend: mirror logic (sell corrective bounces into validated LH zone, stop above LH).

---

## Wicks vs Candle Bodies

- Wicks = rejected price action; show reversal of control (buying failed to sustain, sellers stepped in).
- Candle bodies = confirmed price action; show clear directional control at close.
- A wick over a structural high does NOT validate a HH.
- A wick under a structural low does NOT validate a LL.
- False BOS from wick → often followed by liquidity sweep back the other way (stop hunt).

---

## Liquidity Sweep (Introduced Here, Depth Later)

- A wick over/under a structure level = "massive buying/selling pressure kicking in but failing to close."
- Often sets up traps for traders who trust wick BOS.
- Foreshadows future sweep concept covered in dedicated session.

---

## Risk/Reward Principle

- Buy near HL (low in corrective range) = small risk (stop just below HL), large target (next HH). Good RR.
- Buy at HH = large risk (market can pull all the way to HL), small profit potential. Bad RR.
- A failed trade (HL broken) immediately validates the opposite setup in the new trend direction.

---

## Timeframe Note (Implicit)

- Structure analysis should be done on meaningful highs and lows — not micro-chop.
- "View structure in this broader perspective. High to low focus, not the little chop."
- Wicks alone never define structural highs or lows.
