# When Bitcoin Bottoms

## Gold ↔ BTC Rotation Narrative: Debunked
No cointegration between BTC + gold over 10 years.
- Engle-Granger test p-value = 0.44 (need <0.05 for cointegration)  
- Rolling 2-yr windows: 0/31 periods with cointegration at 5% significance  
- Spread residuals non-stationary

BTC/Gold ratio barely mean-reverts:
- ADF test p = 0.034 (borderline stationary)  
- Half-life ~216 days (7 months)—glacially slow, buried in noise  
- Current BTC ~16oz gold (11% above 14.4 mean); z-score −2.62 (appears "cheap")  
- BUT: driven by gold's parabolic move, not reliable mean-revert force

Gold tops: 2018, 2020, 2022 (only 3 data points). BTC downtrended after 2018+2022; rallied during 2020 risk-on.

**Conclusion**: No fundamental reason BTC + gold move in tandem. Different asset classes.

## BTC as Own Market
Bitcoin ≠ gold safe-haven (50%+ annualized vol vs gold's 15%).  
Bitcoin ≠ stock index (no components, code-based).  
Bitcoin = volatile speculative asset, own regime.

Rotating narratives over years: payments → store of value → digital gold → global reserve → speculation.

## Bottoms: Marked by Derivative Cascades
BTC bottoms characterized by large overreactions across derivatives:
- **Crypto metrics**: open interest drops, negative funding rates  
- **Institutional signals**: options skew (traders overpaying puts), ETF flows  
- **Volatility**: realized vol outpaces implied vol  

Current institutional influx changed landscape:
- CME futures + options in 2023  
- Spot ETFs in 2024 (major accessibility shift)

## Composite Regime Indicator
Track OI + funding + options skew + realized/implied vol in one indicator = depressed market state.

Red zones = bottoming setup:
- Decreasing OI  
- Negative funding  
- Put overpayment  
- Realized vol > implied vol  

Spot-vol correlation increasingly resembles equity indices (less erratic than legacy crypto).

## Entry Rules
Look at **positioning** data—tells best story.  
Monitor derivatives state (OI, funding, skew) for regime confirmation.  
Bottoms form when sentiment exhausted ("everyone given up").

**Avoid**: Trying to catch exact bottom. Use zone-based entries on regime confirmation, not point-prediction.
