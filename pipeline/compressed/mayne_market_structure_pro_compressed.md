## Three Things Price Can Do

1. Uptrend (bullish) — higher highs and higher lows
2. Downtrend (bearish) — lower lows and lower highs
3. Sideways / range — equal highs, equal lows, or no clear discernible direction

Applies on ALL timeframes and ALL asset classes.

---

## Swing High / Swing Low — 3-Candle System

- **Swing high:** 3-candle pattern. Middle candle is the highest; candles on each side are lower.
- **Swing low:** 3-candle pattern. Middle candle is the lowest; candles on each side are higher.
- Must see 3 candles to confirm a swing — nothing is valid until the third candle closes.

---

## Market Structure Break (MSB) = Continuation

- Uptrend MSB: price closes ABOVE a prior swing high → assume uptrend continues → expect higher low.
- Downtrend MSB: price closes BELOW a prior swing low → assume downtrend continues → expect lower high.
- MSB = continuation signal. Not guaranteed, but assumed.
- **Close required.** A wick-only cross is NOT an MSB. (SFP / liquidity sweep distinction.)

---

## Market Structure Shift (MSS) = Potential Reversal

- Uptrend MSS: price closes BELOW the most recent swing low (a higher low is broken).
- Downtrend MSS: price closes ABOVE the most recent swing high (a lower high is broken).
- MSS = potential reversal signal. Not always a reversal, but increased odds.
- Sequence after uptrend MSS: HH then lower low → next bounce puts in lower high → MSB down → downtrend confirmed.

## Regime Classification Rule (HIGHEST PRIORITY FOR BOT)

| Observation | Regime Label | Action |
|---|---|---|
| HH + HL sequence obvious on HTF | Uptrend | Buy pullbacks only |
| LL + LH sequence obvious on HTF | Downtrend | Sell bounces only |
| Equal highs/lows or no clear direction | Range / sideways | Mean-reversion / stand aside |

"If it's not obvious, we're probably going sideways." — visual clarity test is the primary filter.

Ranging price action defined: price pingballs between levels; you can't tell if going up or down; no clear HH-HL or LL-LH progression. Range = trade the range high/low.

---

## Two-Timeframe Framework (HTF + LTF)

**High timeframe (HTF):** provides bias / direction / market structure.
**Low timeframe (LTF):** provides entries, stops, targets.

Rules:
1. Always analyze HTF first.
2. Do NOT take LTF trades against HTF trend.
3. Wait for LTF to sync back up with HTF — that is the highest-probability entry moment.

**Sync moment definition:** LTF was trending counter to HTF; LTF then forms an MSS in the HTF direction; then LTF forms an MSB in the HTF direction. That MSB = entry trigger.

Example: HTF bearish, LTF bullish (pullback). Wait for LTF MSS (breaks a higher low). Then LTF MSB down. NOW enter short. That sync moment generates the strongest / most aggressive moves.

"The moment the H4 flipped bearish, which synced it back up with the daily, the move was 5x faster and larger than the counter-trend grind."

## Key Takeaways (Explicit Stated Rules)

1. Price can only do 3 things: up, down, sideways — on any timeframe.
2. MSB = continuation (assumed). MSS = reversal (potential).
3. HTF leads LTF. Wait for congruence.
4. Market structure should be VISUAL and OBVIOUS. If it isn't, we're probably ranging.
5. MSB requires a candle close through the level. Wick-only = liquidity sweep / SFP.

---

## Ranging Price Action — Practical Description

- No clear HH-HL or LL-LH chain.
- "Pinballing between levels."
- Can be distribution (range before down) or accumulation (range before up) — you cannot tell which in advance.
- Trading approach within range: short range highs, long range lows (mean-reversion).
- No directional bias until MSB or MSS confirms a break.

## Bitcoin Weekly Example (Regime Identification Sequence)

1. Marked first swing low (3-candle), then first swing high (3-candle).
2. Price broke above swing high = MSB → assume uptrend → mark next swing low (= new higher low).
3. Each subsequent MSB refreshes the validated HL and HH pair.
4. Deep pullback within uptrend: valid as long as price doesn't close below validated HL. "We don't freak out."
5. Wick above HH with no close = NOT an MSB. Liquidity sweep. Continue holding bullish.
6. Eventually: MSS fires (price closes below validated HL) → potential reversal. Next bounce = lower high → MSB down → downtrend confirmed.
7. Result: moved from 110k down toward 60k, signaled purely by weekly structure.
