# Trader Mayne — Order Blocks (Compressed)

**Source:** YouTube whiteboard series, ep7 (order blocks)
**Educator:** Trader Mayne

---

## Order Block (OB) — Core Definition

- OB = the last candle (or last consecutive candles) in the OPPOSITE direction before a large displacement move.
- **Bullish OB:** last bearish (down) candle before a large bullish move + MSB.
- **Bearish OB:** last bullish (up) candle before a large bearish move + MSB.
- Draw a box around the entire candle (or candles). Extend it in time.
- When price returns to the zone, expect a reaction / bounce.

---

## Institutional Logic (Stated Rationale)

- Institutions cannot fill entire position in one go; they start filling in the OB zone.
- Price moves away (displacement); remaining orders still resting at OB.
- When price pulls back to OB, those resting orders fill + retail traders who chased are puking out of longs (or shorts) → adding same-direction liquidity.
- OB = institutional fingerprint in the chart.

Note: "Bullish OB = demand zone. Bearish OB = supply zone. A breaker is just an SR flip." Mayne explicitly simplifies ICT jargon to plain concepts.

---

## High-Probability OB Criteria

All increase probability; none are mandatory individually:

1. **Large displacement move after OB** — "the bigger the better." Big candle(s) away from OB = institutional involvement confirmed.
2. **Fair Value Gap (FVG) created by displacement** — gap between candle 1 close and candle 3 open. Sign of displacement. When OB + FVG overlap, double confidence.
3. **Market Structure Break (MSB)** — displacement move closes past a prior swing high/low. Not mandatory but strongly preferred.
4. **Liquidity taken before OB forms** — prior high or low swept first, then OB forms, then displacement. "OB that took liquidity first = super high probability."
5. **OB candle size** — larger body OB candle > tiny OB candle. Can combine adjacent same-direction candles into larger zone if small.

Low probability OB: small displacement, no FVG, no MSB, no liquidity sweep. Can still work, just lower odds.

---

## Breaker Block — Definition

- A breaker = a FAILED order block (SR flip).
- **Bullish breaker:** was a bearish OB; price blasted through it instead of rejecting → zone flips from resistance to support.
- **Bearish breaker:** was a bullish OB; price nuked through it instead of bouncing → zone flips from support to resistance.
- Breakers most commonly occur at trend reversals (MSS points).
- "A breaker is just an SR flip. A bullish OB is just a demand zone. A bearish OB is just a supply zone."

---

## Where OBs and Breakers Tend to Form

- **OBs:** within the existing trend — bullish OBs in uptrends (support), bearish OBs in downtrends (resistance).
- **Breakers:** at the moment trend reverses (MSS). Breaker is often the zone where reversal executes.

OB context rule: "OBs trade with the trend. When the trend reverses, often a breaker."

---

## How to Know if OB Will Fail → Become Breaker

- You cannot know in advance. Assume OB valid until price closes through it.
- Clues that OB may fail (become breaker):
  - Displacement through the zone is NOT present (weak MSB / wick-only break).
  - Context: price at all-time highs with no displacement above = likely liquidity sweep, not real break.
  - Close below (bullish) or above (bearish) the OB after reaching it.
- Once price closes through the OB, it is now a breaker. Reverse bias: use zone as entry in the opposite direction.

---

## Entry Execution — OB Trade

**Bullish OB entry:**
- Enter: at the top of the OB candle down to the 50% (mean threshold) of the OB.
- Layering: can spread limit orders between top and 50%.
- Rule: price should NOT close below the 50% of a high-probability bullish OB. Close below 50% = sign of weakness.
- Stop: below the entire OB (give a few points wiggle for fees/slippage).
- Note: because a good OB first took liquidity, stop below OB is typically safe — the sweep already happened.

**Bearish OB entry:**
- Enter: at the bottom of the OB candle up to the 50% (mean threshold).
- Stop: above the entire OB.

---

## Stop Loss Options for Breaker Entries

Two valid choices (trade-off between win rate and RR):
1. **Tight stop:** just above/below the breaker zone. Better RR when right; stops out more often on wicks.
2. **Wide stop:** above the liquidity sweep high (or below sweep low). Better win rate; worse RR.

Mayne's preference in crypto: wide stop (above sweep) because crypto frequently wicks just past the breaker before reversing.

---

## Targets

- OBs are internal range liquidity.
- Targets = external range liquidity (prior swing highs for bullish OB, prior swing lows for bearish OB).
- "We just hit internal. Target external."

---

## OB Trade Process — Full Workflow

1. **HTF analysis:** determine trend and dealing range (most significant high + low).
2. **Identify liquidity:** external (swing highs/lows) and internal (OB zones, FVGs).
3. **Find OB:** in the discount of range for bullish setups; in premium for bearish setups.
4. **Verify high-probability checklist:** displacement + FVG + MSB + liquidity sweep + OB size.
5. **Entry option A:** enter directly at OB on HTF; stop below OB; target external liquidity. Can be standalone trade.
6. **Entry option B:** zoom into LTF. Within the HTF OB zone, wait for LTF downtrend (pullback) to flip to LTF uptrend (MSS + MSB). Entry = LTF bullish OB or FVG within HTF OB zone. Tighter stop, better RR.

"The confidence we have to get in here with such a tight stop is due to the fact that we know within this gray box we are bullish because it's a high-timeframe bull OB."

---

## OB + FVG Relationship

- Complementary, not competing.
- Displacement (FVG) IS what makes a good OB.
- Best setups: OB and FVG overlap at the same price level → "double the reason, double the confidence."
- OBs, breakers, and FVGs form in the same areas. Future episode: when to use which.

## Key Rules Summary

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| OB definition | Last opposite candle before displacement |
| Bullish OB | Last down candle before big up move + MSB |
| Bearish OB | Last up candle before big down move + MSB |
| Breaker | Failed OB = SR flip |
| Entry zone | Top → 50% of OB (bullish); bottom → 50% (bearish) |
| Close below 50% | Warning sign for bullish OB |
| Stop | Beyond full OB; give wiggle for crypto wicks |
| Target | External range liquidity |
| Best OBs | Took liquidity first + big displacement + FVG + MSB |
| HTF context | Must align; OB in discount (bullish) or premium (bearish) |
