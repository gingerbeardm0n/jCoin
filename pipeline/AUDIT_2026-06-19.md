# jCoin Plan Audit — 2026-06-19

**Reviewer:** Claude (Opus 4.8)
**Scope:** STRATEGY_SPEC.md + 4 TradingRiot concept pages + LESSONS_FROM_JTRADER.md
**Context:** Total beginners to crypto trading, building automated perps mean-reversion bot ($5–25k), learning from public educator content. One educator (TradingRiot) mined so far.

Ordered by threat to the project. Nitpicks omitted.

---

## Killers (resolve before writing engine code)

### 1. Signal data may not exist on the venue you can legally trade
The #1 risk and unchecked. Every concept rests on OI, funding history, liquidation feeds, tick-level delta/CVD. Those feeds historically come from **Binance/Bybit (US-blocked)** or **paid aggregators** (Coinglass, Velo, Laevitas, Amberdata). The liquidation-cascade concept explicitly names "Binance/Bybit liquidation websocket." Build venue = **Coinbase**, whose API gives its own book + trades, not aggregated cross-venue OI/funding/liq.
**Risk:** the entire edge is built on data only available for exchanges you can't use, or behind expensive subscriptions.
**Action:** data-availability audit (see end). If liq/OI isn't gettable for the venue, 3 of 4 concepts collapse.

### 2. Can't backtest these signals → can't validate any parameter
Corollary to #1. Parity principle (jTrader lesson #1) requires same data in backtest = sim = live. Validating `z=2.0`, `funding 0.10%`, `oi_drop 3%` needs *historical* OI/funding/liq/tick-delta series — short (perps young, few regimes) and hard to source. Without replay, every threshold stays a guess and you tune live with real money.

### 3. Every parameter is one educator's vibe, zero fills behind it
Concept pages say it: "0 verified fills," "win rate not stated," many numbers tagged *inferred* or single-source (stop 0.3×ATR invented; absorption delta 10% from one article). jTrader lesson literally: *don't encode an educator's vibe as a parameter.* Current corpus = 100% that. **Treat concept pages as a test backlog (hypotheses), not a spec.**

### 4. Mean-reversion-first may be the wrong first build for beginners
Strategic. Fading extremes has an *unbounded* failure mode — short into the thing that keeps ripping. Spec itself calls the trend-trap "the strategy's killer." Hardest-to-survive, hardest-to-verify style chosen as build #1. Breakout/momentum (jTrader DNA) has *bounded* risk — stopped out fast and cheap. Mean-reversion was chosen for maker-fee economics, but fee savings are irrelevant if the tail blows the account. **Reconsider build order.**

### 5. Maker-fill paradox poisons mean-reversion specifically
Maker-first (resting limit at zone) for fees. But a resting limit at an extreme fills *precisely when price punches through it* — filled on moves that DON'T revert, missing the ones that do. **Adverse selection** — systematic, not bad luck. Maker + fade = the exact combo where "free" fills are the losing fills. Compounds #4.

## Serious (fixable in design)

### 6. Spec contradicts itself
Spec §3 "Explicitly NOT building" lists *liquidation-cascade hunting — highest blowup risk.* Yet `concept_liquidation_cascade_reversal` is one of the four. "Reversal after" vs "hunting during" may be a real distinction — but reconcile explicitly or cut the concept.

### 7. Mandate drift — set out to scalp, produced swing trades
Mandate = "momentum scalping, many small trades." Concepts = opposite: cascade reversal "10-day forward return, hold up to 10 days"; VWAP fade "hours to days"; profile fade session-based. Holding perps for days = repeated funding + overnight liquidation/gap risk. Either the mandate changes (fine) or the strategy does — right now doc and rules disagree.

### 8. Regime filter is the linchpin and least-specified
Every concept survives on "only in range, skip trends." That filter is the one thing between you and the catastrophic tail (#4). No concept page for it — no ADX value, no vol-of-vol definition. Most important piece, least rigor. **The regime classifier needs its own concept page and the hardest validation, before the setups that depend on it.**

## Worth noting

### 9. Correlation = hidden leverage
BTC/ETH perps ~0.8+ correlated; mean-reversion fades all fire at the same market-wide extreme. Two 0.5% fades on correlated legs = ~1% on one bet, failing together. Risk framework caps notional but ignores signal correlation. Add "one theme at a time" or correlation-adjusted sizing.

### 10. Overfit surface large, validation data thin
~28 params, mostly single-sourced point estimates, against short perps history with few regimes. Even "v1 ~14" = 14 guesses. Optuna will overfit hard. Keep v1 to **6–8 params**, freeze the rest until data earns them.

### 11. "Educator consensus" is currently one voice
TradingRiot sells a course; free content is the funnel — directionally honest, edge often simplified/withheld. Mayne + Clark not mined yet. Don't treat concepts as near-final until cross-source agreement exists.

## What's good (keep)
Pipeline architecture — provenance tracking, codeable flags, decision-rule output, param-counting vs cap. Fee-first principle. Venue-agnostic design. The learning *machine* is sound; the *inputs and sequencing* need work.
