# jCoin audit — 2026-10-23

Scope: everything since the forum outreach began (commit `4212db9`, 2026-10-12) through `0520ef8` (2026-10-22), plus the live VM, the paper state, the `community/` folder and the community-hub handoff.
Method: orchestrator plus 10 read-only scouts (8 in wave 1, 2 in wave 2), with direct VM checks through Cloud Shell. Every HIGH item was confirmed by at least two independent sources.

Terms used below:
- **R** = one unit of the risk taken on a trade. −1R means the stop was hit.
- **PF** = profit factor (gross wins ÷ gross losses).
- **DD** = drawdown.
- **MAR** = annual return ÷ max drawdown.
- **ATR** = average true range (typical bar size).
- **CAGR** = compound annual growth rate.

---

## 1. Verdict

jCoin is not ready for real money. You already knew that, and the research discipline behind it is real: the pre-registration commit order checks out.

The audit sharpens why it isn't ready:
- **Strategy A's whole profit comes from about 10 giant trend trades in 5.4 years.** Its top 10 of 396 trades are 109% of total R. Without them, the average trade is −0.05R.
- **The venue you picked appears to clip exactly those trades.** Coinbase nano prints rare 1–2% spikes, and its stops trigger on last-trade price, with no mark or index option.
- **Paper trading cannot settle this in any reasonable time.** Telling a real edge from luck takes about 270–330 trades, which is about 5 years at 5 trades a month.

**The single thing that matters most:** find out whether A's big winners survive on Coinbase, using historical data now (spot- or index-referenced stops) plus a per-trade parity ledger during paper. Stop testing new strategies. Four challengers since 10-14 all lost, and none of them touched the real bottleneck, which is execution on the venue.

The ops changes shipped yesterday (my work) have a broken safety gate. Fix that first, because it is cheap.

## 2. What we're doing wrong (ranked)

### W1. The auto-update gate is broken. No future commit will ever deploy. [HIGH, verified 2×]
- **Evidence:**
  - pytest on the VM gives `1 failed, 81 passed`. The failure is `tests/test_carver_trend.py::test_daily_breakout_produces_trades`, raising `FileNotFoundError` because `data/` is gitignored and absent on the VM. I ran this directly on the VM, and it reproduces on a clean clone.
  - Every push will merge, fail the tests, roll back, and retry hourly forever, with no alert.
  - Also broken:
    - `pip install -e .` is a no-op error, because there is no pyproject or setup.py. New requirements are never installed.
    - The merge-failure branch is unreachable under `set -e`.
    - The VM tree is dirty because of a `chmod +x` mode change on `auto-update.sh`, so any commit touching that file fails to merge.
    - Only ccxt is pinned. The VM runs polars 2.0.0.
- **Why it matters:** you would believe fixes are live when they aren't.
- **Confidence:** high.

### W2. The validated strategy is not the code that trades. [HIGH, verified 2×]
- **Evidence:**
  - The live engine caps position notional at 1× equity (`risk.py:26,55-62`). The research harness that produced every decision number has no cap (`portfolio_backtest.py:211`).
  - The cap binds on 9% of entries, and those are the best trades: they average 1.79R, and they include the 43.5R winner.
  - On the same dev data, the engine makes 29.8% CAGR against the headline 35.4–35.6%.
  - The live bot also has a "cycle filter" (0.75× risk when BTC is below its 365-day average) that no validation run includes. It costs about 1% CAGR. Its justification ("bear PF ~1.59", `STRATEGY_SPEC.md:212`) has no source in the repo.
- **Why it matters:** the head-to-head, the Monte Carlo, the kill rule and the public numbers all describe a slightly different, better bot than the one running.
- **Confidence:** high.

### W3. The edge is thin and tail-dependent, and the docs overstate how clean it is. [HIGH, verified 2×]
- **Evidence:**
  - Avg R is 0.527 with t ≈ 2.2 and a 95% CI of [0.09, 1.03]. Win rate is **17.9%**, not the "~25%" in `STRATEGY_SPEC.md:146` and the Reddit post.
  - Top 10 trades = 109% of total R.
  - `HEAD_TO_HEAD_2026-10.md:39-41` says A "wins 2022–2024, which predate" the trail-6.0 choice. That is false: trail 6.0 came from a full-range 2021–26 grid of 432 configs (`VALIDATION_RULES.md:34`), and 6.0 sits at the grid edge.
  - The sealed "CONFIRMED" look had about 3 truly blind trades out of 18, against a pass bar (DD < 21.8%) that was nearly impossible to fail.
- **Why it matters:** expect real-world results well below the backtest. Any execution effect that trims big winners erases the edge entirely.
- **Confidence:** high.

### W4. Coinbase stop mechanics can't avoid the spike problem natively. [HIGH, external + repo]
- **Evidence:**
  - The Advanced Trade API offers only `stop_limit` and `trigger_bracket`, both triggered on last trade. There is no stop-market and no mark or index trigger ([create-order docs](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/orders/create-order)).
  - The exchange's price protections only act on moves of 5% or more ([CFTC filing](https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/filings/ptc/25/06/ptc06262524818.pdf)).
  - PF on Coinbase prices was 0.59 vs 1.12 on Binance over the same 10 months.
  - On ~70 trades that gap is not statistically conclusive alone: 60% of it is one trade. But the mechanism (the trailing stop ratchets on a single spike high) is plausible, and no rolling 70-trade window in dev history ever fell that low.
- **Why it matters:** this is the binding constraint on going live.
- **Confidence:** medium-high.

### W5. The kill rule is a disaster stop, not an edge alarm, and the public claim about it was wrong. [HIGH, verified 2×]
- **Evidence:**
  - At 5 trades a month, a strategy that loses like the Coinbase backtest (PF 0.59) takes a **median of about 23 months** to trip 39.8% DD or 33 losses. A zero-edge strategy takes about 46 months. The earliest possible trip is about 7 months.
  - It was calibrated on uncapped 1%-risk harness trades using realized equity. The real bot is capped, runs 0.7–2.4% risk from lot rounding, and DD is realized-only (`KILL_RULE.md` caveat).
  - It was written 10-14, after 4 paper losses (09-21 ×2, 09-29, 10-08). The thresholds come purely from the backtest, so they were not fitted to the losses. But my hub answer (`community-hub/inbox/jcoin_2026-10-22.md`) says "committed before paper results", and hub draft `followups_2026-10-22.md` #4 repeats it.
- **Why it matters:** the rule won't save you from a venue that quietly kills the edge, and an unposted public claim is false.
- **Confidence:** high.

### W6. Monitoring is blind to a frozen feed, and there is no state backup. [HIGH, verified 2×]
- **Evidence:**
  - "No new closed bar" and "FEED STALE" both exit 0 and ping healthchecks green (`run_paper.py:101-109`, `paper_runner.py:286-301`). The healthchecks URL **is** set on the VM. I checked.
  - The only staleness watchdog is the Windows one, and those laptop tasks are now disabled.
  - `state.json` (the paper clock) is not backed up.
  - If it is lost, the bot silently replays about 2,300 bars from a **$10,000** default start (`run_paper.py:42`). That can latch the kill switch on fake trades.
- **Why it matters:** the bot can be dead for days while showing green.
- **Confidence:** high.

### W7. Sizing at $1–2k is "1 contract or none". [MED-HIGH, verified 2× + external]
- **Evidence:**
  - 1 BTC nano is 0.01 BTC, about $820–840.
  - At $928 equity, a 1×ATR stop risks 0.8–2.5% instead of 1%. A trade is skipped when 1 contract exceeds 2× the target risk.
  - BTC becomes untradeable below about $815 equity, because of the notional cap.
  - Skipped or rounded signals are never logged: the logger has no handler, and `engine.py:71` is silent.
  - Carver says 2 instruments is "unlikely to be enough" and wants at least 4 contracts at max forecast ([post](https://qoppac.blogspot.com/2016/03/diversification-and-small-account-size.html)).
- **Why it matters:** the account size is at the floor of feasibility, and actual risk is lumpy.
- **Confidence:** high.

### W8. The Carver and daily-bar research (10-21) is invalid as reported, and it has already been relayed to others. [MED, verified 3×]
- **Evidence:**
  - Carver: no forecast scalar (mean |forecast| ≈ 1–2 instead of ~10), and ATR used as volatility. The result is about 3% average exposure, which is why CAGR came out under 1% (`carver_trend.py:79-93,176-179`). Corrected, it is still no better than buy-and-hold on 2 coins and loses at spot costs.   - `A_DAILY` hard-codes wrong A baseline numbers (`carver_trend.py:414`: 30.1/1.10/1.53 instead of 32.4/1.24/1.60).
  - `max_hold` was not rescaled for daily bars (120 days).
  - "Weaker than 4h" is not statistically supported (t = 0.92).
  - Neither test was pre-registered or logged in the Looks Log. The tests are shallow.
  - My hub answer cited the invalid 0.9% figure, and per the docs scout it was posted to iSnake37 on 10-23.
- **Confidence:** high on the bug. The corrected numbers come from one scout's replica.

## 4. What we're probably fine on (verified)
- Pre-registration integrity: rule commits precede result commits and scripts (`4d123d8`, `7f883ca`, `931c8e3`). Edits are additive.
- No look-ahead found in the harnesses (swings confirm at i+k, the universe uses past bars only). There is a minor ~4h funding-window offset.
- Paper logic reproduces backtest trades bar-for-bar on the same price data (trades 1-6 match within 0.006R).
- **The 7 straight losers are normal.** At a 17.9% win rate, P(7 non-wins) ≈ 25%. The dev history's longest streak was 22. All 7 were initial-stop exits, which is also typical (316 of 325 dev losers were).
- The kill-rule counters count only since the reset (nonwin_streak 7, peak $1,000). The latch survives a restart.
- Funding accrual sign and units are correct (hourly, close-bar notional, no double count).
- No secrets in the repo, its history, `community/` or community-hub. `.env` was never committed.
- The VM cannot place real orders: the unit runs `--mode paper`, there is no Coinbase broker, and the Kraken path is demo-only.
- The VM is healthy: up 9 days, 664 MB RAM free, disk at 17%, timer firing hourly, healthchecks URL set.
- `state.json` writes are atomic, and the restart timing (update at :00, tick at :05) is safe.
- The sudoers line is narrowly scoped.
- Coinbase 4h bars are native and UTC-aligned, and the forming bar is dropped.
- A beat B fairly in the head-to-head. The Sharpe-method differences don't change the decision.

## 5. Blind spots
- No live Coinbase order-book depth or spread data. FCM fees unknown. The US UI stop options page returned 403 (the API docs were checked).
- The "spikes cause the gap" mechanism is plausible but unproven. No one has winsorized wicks or injected spikes.
- The corrected Carver numbers and the kill-rule trip-time simulations each come from a single scout.
- Branch protection on `main` and deploy-key permissions could not be read (no admin token).
- The Kraken broker order path was not deeply audited. There is no live Coinbase code to audit yet.
- Kraken US perps (via Bitnomial, launched June 2026) API availability is unverified. It might be a deeper-book alternative venue.
- The community-hub session's own process was audited only through jCoin files. jSwing and jTrader are out of scope.
